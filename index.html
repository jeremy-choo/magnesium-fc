Our story

It started on a couch.

During the Melbourne lockdowns, Magnesium FC started with just three of us playing FIFA Pro Clubs. Once we could get back outside, five-a-side futsal brought a crew together in real life, and we haven't stopped growing since.

More people wanted to play, so the club kept growing with them.

How we grew

  1. Pro ClubsThree mates, a console and a lockdown.
  2. Five-a-side futsalThe crew meets in real life. Four futsal championships follow.
  3. Seven-a-sideMore people want in. We win the 2024 Knox 7s.
  4. Eleven-a-sideWe play every Sunday in the MCSA league. This is where you come in.
